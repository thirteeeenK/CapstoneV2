## Boracay Ocean Club Beach Resort (10 reviews, 4-5★)

- Staff members are consistently praised as superb, polite, and highly amenable to guest requests to ensure safety.
- Guests highlight the comfortable and luxurious hotel experience, noting the excellent breakfast buffet and rooftop pool entertainment.
- The property boasts an exceptional location at Station 3, making it a perfect spot on the beach that is highly enjoyable for families with kids.
- Amenities offer great value, with reviewers appreciating the inclusion of airport transfers and the convenience of having multiple amenities in one hotel.

## Miniloc Island (10 reviews, 4.65-5★)

- The staff are described as cheerful, kind, and meticulous in taking care of every detail to make guests feel welcome.
- Facilities include incredibly clean rooms and a natural cove located right in front of the hotel for easy snorkeling with sea turtles and fish.
- Guests appreciate the all-inclusive tours, such as the exclusive 6 AM Big Lagoon tour, which allows for various experiences without needing a wallet.
- The fresh, daily-changing meals and pristine natural environment create a highly satisfying, paradise-like resort experience.

## Cauayan Island Resort (10 reviews, 4.7-5★)

- Reviewers consistently mention the VIP treatment, world-class service, and lovely staff who ensure a stress-free stay.
- The beachfront villas are stunning and well-furnished, offering incredible views of the bay with private sunbeds.
- Hotel-offered excursions, snorkeling with thousands of fish, and nighttime massages are highlighted as top-notch experiences.
- While considered an expensive and indulgent escape, the delicious food, excellent facilities, and overall paradise setting make it worth the cost.

## Spin Designer Hostel (10 reviews, 4.4-4.8★)

- The hostel features a cool, modern design with highly inviting common areas and very clean bathrooms that are serviced multiple times a day.
- Amenities exceed standard hostel expectations, including a good free breakfast of pancakes and eggs, reliable Wi-Fi, and rare hot water showers with strong pressure.
- The social atmosphere is a major draw, with staff organizing engaging events like BBQ nights and pub crawls to help guests meet other travelers.
- It offers a highly convenient location just a short walk away from the main beach and local restaurants.

## Happiness Hostel

- Guests praise the social party vibe, with a fun downstairs bar, boat parties, and staff-organized events making it easy to meet people.
- Beds are commended as comfortable with privacy curtains, and bathrooms are consistently described as very clean with working air conditioning.
- The location earns frequent praise for its easy walk to the beach and nearby clubs, though nights can get loud as expected in a party hostel.
- Staff receive strong marks for helpfulness, especially with booking tours, and the on-site restaurant food surprises guests positively

## Henann Palm Beach Resort

- Staff are consistently described as warm, kind, and attentive, delivering seamless service from check-in to check-out.
- Rooms earn praise as spotless, modern, and comfortable with clean beds and fluffy bath towels.
- Guests highlight the beachfront location near D'Mall, the sky and regular pools, and the beautiful beach right in front.
- The breakfast buffet draws repeated compliments, capping stays guests describe as exceeding expectations and worth every peso.
