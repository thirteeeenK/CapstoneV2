## Boracay Ocean Club Beach Resort (10 reviews)

- Staff members are consistently praised as superb, polite, and highly amenable to guest requests to ensure safety.
- Guests highlight the comfortable and luxurious hotel experience, noting the excellent breakfast buffet and rooftop pool entertainment.
- The property boasts an exceptional location at Station 3, making it a perfect spot on the beach that is highly enjoyable for families with kids.
- Amenities offer great value, with reviewers appreciating the inclusion of airport transfers and the convenience of having multiple amenities in one hotel.

## Miniloc Island (10 reviews)

- The staff are described as cheerful, kind, and meticulous in taking care of every detail to make guests feel welcome.
- Facilities include incredibly clean rooms and a natural cove located right in front of the hotel for easy snorkeling with sea turtles and fish.
- Guests appreciate the all-inclusive tours, such as the exclusive 6 AM Big Lagoon tour, which allows for various experiences without needing a wallet.
- The fresh, daily-changing meals and pristine natural environment create a highly satisfying, paradise-like resort experience.

## Cauayan Island Resort (10 reviews)

- Reviewers consistently mention the VIP treatment, world-class service, and lovely staff who ensure a stress-free stay.
- The beachfront villas are stunning and well-furnished, offering incredible views of the bay with private sunbeds.
- Hotel-offered excursions, snorkeling with thousands of fish, and nighttime massages are highlighted as top-notch experiences.
- While considered an expensive and indulgent escape, the delicious food, excellent facilities, and overall paradise setting make it worth the cost.

## Spin Designer Hostel (10 reviews)

- The hostel features a cool, modern design with highly inviting common areas and very clean bathrooms that are serviced multiple times a day.
- Amenities exceed standard hostel expectations, including a good free breakfast of pancakes and eggs, reliable Wi-Fi, and rare hot water showers with strong pressure.
- The social atmosphere is a major draw, with staff organizing engaging events like BBQ nights and pub crawls to help guests meet other travelers.
- It offers a highly convenient location just a short walk away from the main beach and local restaurants.

## Happiness Hostel

- Guests praise the social party vibe, with a fun downstairs bar, boat parties, and staff-organized events making it easy to meet people.
- Beds are commended as comfortable with privacy curtains, and bathrooms are consistently described as very clean with working air conditioning.
- The location earns frequent praise for its easy walk to the beach and nearby clubs, though nights can get loud as expected in a party hostel.
- Staff receive strong marks for helpfulness, especially with booking tours, and the on-site restaurant food surprises guests positively

## Henann Palm Beach Resort

- Staff are consistently described as warm, kind, and attentive, delivering seamless service from check-in to check-out.
- Rooms earn praise as spotless, modern, and comfortable with clean beds and fluffy bath towels.
- Guests highlight the beachfront location near D'Mall, the sky and regular pools, and the beautiful beach right in front.
- The breakfast buffet draws repeated compliments, capping stays guests describe as exceeding expectations and worth every peso.

## Canyon Hotels & Resorts Boracay (10 reviews)

- Guests repeatedly describe the staff as friendly, accommodating, and professional, with service that makes the stay feel comfortable and welcoming.
- The rooms and common areas are frequently characterized as clean, neat, and comfortable, while several reviews also mention reliable Wi-Fi, hot water, and a pool.
- Its location near Robinsons Mall and the complimentary shuttle to Station 2 are viewed as practical conveniences for getting around Boracay.
- Reviewers commonly see the property as a good-value, budget-friendly choice that still provides attentive service and useful facilities.

## Henann Garden Resort (10 reviews)

- Guests consistently commend the pleasant, helpful staff and the quick service provided from check-in onward.
- Rooms are described as spacious, modern, clean, and comfortable, while the pool area is also noted as tidy and enjoyable.
- The resort's ambience, accessible Station 2 location, and proximity to D'Mall and the beach make it appealing for family vacations.
- Several reviews portray the stay as comfortable and dependable enough to encourage repeat visits.

## My Station Hotel (10 reviews)

- Reviewers most often value the hotel as an affordable, practical place to stay in Station 1, only a short walk from the beachfront.
- Rooms are generally described as basic but cozy and clean, with working air conditioning and cable TV mentioned by some guests.
- Staff receive positive feedback for being accommodating, attentive, and responsive to guest requests.
- The overall impression is of a convenient, budget-friendly option for travelers who prioritize location and value.

## Frendz Resort & Hostel (10 reviews)

- The hostel is praised for its lively, social atmosphere, where guests can meet travelers from different places and join activities around the bar and pasta nights.
- Friendly staff are a recurring strength, with reviewers noting their helpfulness and the welcoming atmosphere they create.
- Guests describe the rooms as clean for a hostel and the beds as comfortable.
- Its Station 2 location, a few minutes from the beach, and its reasonable value make it a popular choice for social travelers.

## Lazy Dog Bed & Breakfast (10 reviews)

- Guests describe the property as a quiet, relaxing retreat away from Boracay's busier areas, while still being within walking distance of D'Mall.
- The Bulabog Beach location is appreciated by guests who enjoy kitesurfing, and the pet-friendly setting and courtyard add to the relaxed character.
- Rooms are commonly described as clean, cozy, and distinctively designed, while the included breakfast receives positive remarks.
- Reviewers also highlight the accommodating staff and the strong value offered for the price.

## The Muse Hotel (10 reviews)

- Guests praise the boutique property's attractive, modern interiors and clean facilities, often describing it as a luxury-feeling stay.
- The location near Station 1 beach is repeatedly valued for convenience, while the rooftop pool and jacuzzi are popular places to enjoy the sunset.
- Rooms are noted as spacious and comfortable, with especially favorable comments about the beds.
- Attentive staff, welcome touches on arrival, and a varied breakfast buffet contribute to an overall positive experience.

## El Nido Resorts - Pangulasian Island (10 reviews)

- Reviewers portray the resort as a high-end island escape with striking views, comfortable villas, and a consistently polished level of service.
- Staff are praised for being friendly, attentive, and accommodating, including in handling dietary requests.
- Snorkeling directly from the beach, complimentary boat trips, private cruises, and island views are recurring highlights of the stay.
- Guests also commend the varied food, strong attention to detail, and the resort's eco-friendly practices.

## Seda Lio (10 reviews)

- The resort is valued for its quiet, attractive Lio Estate setting near the airport, with a shuttle that makes trips to El Nido town easier.
- Guests frequently mention the large pool, direct access to a clean beach, and open spaces that work well for families.
- Rooms are described as clean, modern, and comfortable, while the beach and wider property are considered well maintained.
- Professional staff, a broad breakfast selection, and nearby Lio shops add to the convenience of the stay.

## Charlie's El Nido (10 reviews)

- Guests describe the hotel as clean, comfortable, peaceful, and thoughtfully designed, with a well-maintained pool area.
- The staff are regularly commended for going the extra mile and making visitors feel welcome throughout the stay.
- Complimentary airport transfers and shuttle service are useful conveniences, especially given the short drive from the airport.
- Reviewers also mention the breakfast and restaurant food positively, presenting the hotel as an easy and relaxing base in El Nido.

## Panorama Resort (10 reviews)

- Guests appreciate the boutique design, clean and comfortable rooms, and quieter Corong-Corong location away from the main town's noise.
- The staff are often described as proactive and welcoming, particularly when arranging tours and transport.
- Sunset views from the beach-club area and the attractive pool are among the property's most memorable features.
- Food is considered delicious despite being relatively expensive, while one review notes that Wi-Fi can be slow.

## Buko Beach Resort (10 reviews)

- Reviewers consistently highlight the friendly, responsive staff and the careful attention given to guests during their stay.
- The food, included breakfast, clean rooms, and comfortable accommodations receive repeated positive comments.
- Guests describe the small resort as quiet and relaxing, with appealing sunset views, private beach access, and attractive villas.
- The private-pool villa and the property's intimate, well-designed atmosphere are recurring reasons guests recommend it.

## Lihim Resorts (10 reviews)

- Guests repeatedly praise the warm, attentive service, including butler assistance and a level of hospitality they describe as exceptional.
- The villas, facilities, and jungle-and-beach setting are regarded as beautiful, well maintained, and suited to a peaceful getaway.
- Boat access to the beach and to El Nido adds a distinctive part of the experience for several visitors.
- Reviewers generally consider the resort a high-quality, worthwhile choice for couples and families seeking privacy and relaxation.

## Matinloc Resort (10 reviews)

- Guests are especially impressed by the natural scenery, ocean views, beaches, and opportunities to see fish directly in front of the resort.
- The villas are described as spacious and luxurious, with comfortable interiors and memorable views from family and beach accommodations.
- Staff are praised for being friendly, responsive, and helpful with transportation arrangements.
- The large pool, food quality, and calm atmosphere round out reviews that frame the property as a scenic and relaxing island retreat.
